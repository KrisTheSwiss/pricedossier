Methodology

Three steps to a verified audit

Every market check uses a residential IP in that country, not a data centre. What we observe is what your customer sees.

01

You share your pricing URL

One link, plus the countries you want covered. No integration, no access credentials, no onboarding required.

02

We verify from each market

Residential proxies simulate a real browser in each country. Exit-IP confirmation proves the request originated there. Prices are extracted and cross-checked.

03

You receive the report

A presentation-ready PDF within 48 hours. Pricing by market, PPP-adjusted targets, delta analysis, and prioritised recommendations.
